## It Was Tall 0.4.6

### New
- **Every player starts with an old radio.** On your first join you get one, and a chest with two more radios stands right by the world spawn.
- **Your radio is heard by the players around you.** Its broadcasts and static come from you and follow you as you walk (within 16 blocks). You hear your own radio like music, the same in both ears (Ambient volume slider).
- **Walkie-talkie** *(needs Simple Voice Chat)*. It is not an item: every player has an extra slot to the right of the hotbar. Select it with **0** or by scrolling past the last hotbar slot, then **hold right click to talk**. Everyone out of normal voice range hears you crackling like a radio, with a squelch click when you key up. The slot shows **TX** while you talk and **RX + name** while someone else does.
- **It steals voices** *(needs Simple Voice Chat, off by default: `itMimicsVoices` in the config)*. It keeps short pieces of what players say, in memory only, and at night plays them back from the dark behind you: slowed down, too deep, with an echo. Your friend's voice from the trees, while your friend is somewhere else.

### Changed
- Radio sounds no longer stay behind where they started playing.
- The radio key is now **I** (was R).

### Recently added (0.4.5)
- **God mode** for sessions with friends (`/changetogod`, operators): fly unseen, open the God Panel with **G**, fire any event at any player, control stage, time and weather, see everyone on a live mini-map, and **play as It** (H): rise from the ground, hunt a player by looking at them, catch them.
- **It speaks**: 24 distorted voice lines ("I'm here.", "What are you mining here?").
- **The radio** item, **the blood moon** (a red night when everything comes twice as often), **the corrupted world** around your base, **the Mimics** (bloodied animals with wrong sounds), **the Eyes** between the trees, **the Hanged** on a tree, **the chopping block** and other night scenes.

Simple Voice Chat is optional: without it everything else works, the walkie-talkie slot just says it needs the mod.
