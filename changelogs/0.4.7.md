## It Was Tall 0.4.7 — Icons Update (beta)

### New HUD
- **Heartbeat.** An anatomical heart next to your health bar contracts with every beat. Your pulse rises with the tension of the night and races when It is near. The BPM shows from 100, in red from 130.
- **Clock.** A sun or moon with the time left until dusk (or dawn). The last 30 seconds pulse red.
- **Compass to your bed.** A needle turns toward your base, with the distance next to it. It turns red when night comes and you are far away.
- **Shelter.** A little house whose window lights up while you count as inside (in your base or under a roof).
- **Stage icon.** It gets a little closer in every stage: from an empty horizon to a face right in front of you.
- **Last words.** The death screen shows the last line of your journal, and a red **IT WAS TALL.** when It was what killed you.

### New look
- Hand-drawn 16×16 pixel icons in the journal's colours (parchment, ink, dried blood).
- One shared leather frame for every HUD piece, the radio panel too.
- Pieces slide in from the edge and fade out. The house window fades on like a lantern.
- The HUD moves below the potion icons when you have effects. Everything is steady (no pulsing) in photosensitive mode, and `hudExtras = false` in the config hides it all.

### Playing as It (god mode)
- You no longer see the It body you are wearing.
- Heavy footsteps follow your walking, with now and then a growl, a sniff or a breath that everyone around can hear.
- **It's eyes:** a blood-red, grainy view with a breathing vignette and night vision. The edges pulse red while you hunt someone.
