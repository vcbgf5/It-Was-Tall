## It Was Tall 0.4.8 (beta)

### New HUD
- **The eye.** At the top of the screen an eye slowly opens while It is watching you, even when you cannot see it. It blinks now and then and trembles wide open when It is about to come for you.
- **IT IS COMING.** During a chase the eye turns into a torn red bar that shrinks as It closes in.
- **NIGHT 3.** At dusk the night number is typed in the middle of the screen, letter by letter, like on a typewriter.
- **Saving.** A quill writes on a sheet of parchment in the bottom-right corner whenever the game is saved. Achievements, new journal lines, shared notes and finished tasks now save the mod's data and your advancements right away, like checkpoints.
- **Polaroids.** The first time you clearly see It, the hanged one, a carcass, the Deep One or the Old One, the moment is photographed. The photo, in faded instant-film colours, is pinned to the left of the screen with a handwritten line, develops from black and slides away.

### For testing
- `/itwastall debug recordvoice [from] [target]` records the next 5 seconds of a player's voice (Simple Voice Chat), then It plays exactly that back near the target. Also in the god panel: "Record 5 s + play".

### Fixes
- **Compass needle.** It now turns around its own centre instead of drifting to the side.
- **Walkie-talkie hint.** Shorter ("hold RMB to talk"), so it no longer runs off the right edge of the screen.
- **Radio panel.** Everything now fits inside the frame: ON AIR, ON / OFF and the level meter keep a margin from the edges.
- **Walkie-talkie slot.** Uses the same leather frame as the rest of the HUD, with a red border while it is selected.

### Playing as It (god mode)
- **See yourself as It.** In third person (F5) you now see the tall body you are wearing, moving and turning with you. In first person it stays hidden so it does not block your view.

### NeoForge (unofficial)
- The mod can be run on **NeoForge 1.21.1** through **Sinytra Connector** (with Forgified Fabric API and GeckoLib for NeoForge). This is not tested by us, and the Simple Voice Chat features (walkie-talkie, radio voices, It copying voices) do not work there. Please report anything that breaks.
