## It Was Tall 0.4.9 (beta)

### For testing
- `/itwastall debug mimicvoicenow [player]` makes It play the newest recorded voice right now, behind the player, even with `itMimicsVoices` off. Record one first with `/itwastall debug recordvoice`.

### The Below
- **There is something under the bedrock.** Endless dark caves between a bedrock floor and a bedrock ceiling: big hollow chambers, winding tunnels, deepslate and tuff, dead sculk, black water, a black fog after a few dozen blocks.
- **The way in:** fall into the void under the Overworld (you no longer die there - you land in the caves), or break through **Cracked Bedrock**. From stage 2 it now and then appears in the floor of the deepest mines, with digging heard from under it. An iron pickaxe breaks it, slowly.
- **Down there:** dripping, falling stones, footsteps that are not yours, breathing right behind you, digging far away. Bones, skulls, cobwebs, burnt-out candles and the cold camps of those who came before (with a chest). And It, standing at the far end of a cave, watching.
- **The way out:** live until dawn, or die. Either way you wake up in your bed, and you keep everything you carried.
- Two new advancements: **Under Everything** and **Dawn Down There**.
- Config: `below`, `belowMinStage`, `crackedBedrockMinutes`. Commands: `/itwastall event below` and `/itwastall event crackedbedrock`; both are also in the god panel.

### Advancements
- Every advancement now has its own hand-drawn pixel icon from the new icon pack.
- Advancement names and descriptions are shown in English in every language.
