## It Was Tall 0.5.0 - The Last Night (beta)

### The ending
- **It can be sent away.** Somewhere in **The Below** stand **Forgotten Archives**: rooms of carved stone with a chest holding the **Ritual Plan** (a sketch of the ritual, from above and from the side) and the **Forgotten Book** (what the ones who came before built from the stones down there). The first time you go down, an archive is always close by.
- **Old altars** deep in the Below hold the **Hollow Heart**, the offering. Sometimes one lies in a cold camp's chest too.
- **The ritual:** a burning campfire on a 3x3 floor of Oldrock Bricks, four Ritual Pillars (two high) three blocks from it with a lit candle on each. At night, give the Hollow Heart to the fire.
- **The Last Night:** fog, corrupted music, whispers. It comes again and again, and blows the candles out. If any part of the ritual stays broken for 15 seconds, it fails and It is still here.
- **Dawn:** if the ritual still stands when the sun comes up, the ritual ends it: from every pillar a chain grows, link by link, to a point above the fire. There It appears, lying on its back in the air, arms, legs and head hanging down, and the chains lift it slowly into the morning sky. The fire goes out, the ground glows red and a pentagram turns on it. The camera leaves you for it: a cutscene shows the fire from the front, It rising from the side and the pentagram from above. Then It is gone, the ending plays — the credits roll over dark dirt like the game's own, with the Sleeper talking to you — and the world is free: no more haunting, events, scares or voices. `/itwastall phase activate` brings it back.
- New advancements: **The Last Night** and **It Was Tall. It Is Gone.**

### New blocks
- Forgotten Stone Bricks, Oldrock Bricks, Carved Forgotten Stone, Ritual Pillar, Oldrock Lantern (bright). Recipes are in the Forgotten Book (and in the recipe book).

### Fog nights
- Now and then (stage 2+) a night comes with a thick grey fog: you see a dozen blocks. Heavy footsteps walk around you in it, and It stands at the edge of what you can see.

### Corrupted music
- The game's own songs sometimes play wrong: slowed down, warbling like a stretched tape, dropping out, stopping and starting again. More often in every stage, always on fog nights. Config `corruptedMusic`.

### Settings screen
- Every option of the config in a screen, with its description as a tooltip. Open it from **Mod Menu** or with the new **Settings** button in the pause menu.

### For testing
- `/itwastall event fog`, `/itwastall event ritual` (builds the whole ritual in front of you and gives you a Hollow Heart), `/itwastall event finale` (the ritual's end and the ending, only for you), `/itwastall event ending` (only the ending screen), `/itwastall event archive`, `/itwastall event altar`. Also in the god panel.
